WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Mira-Bhayander's Most Loved Tea Destination.

“9 Tea's Cafe” was founded in 2019 at Bhayander East, Thane by passionate young entrepreneurs Amit Indrabali Yadav & Rupesh Govind Pendhari. Driven by the deep-rooted cultural love for Indian tea and the carefree warmth of the 90s, the cafe immediately resonated with the local youth, students, and working professionals.

Guided by the mentorship of C.A. Prashant Tripathi (Founder of Elite Education), the founders set out to build a scalable, standardized, and high-quality Indian cafe brand that elevates everyday street chai into a premium, hygienic, smoke-free cafe experience without losing its authentic soul.

Today, 9Tea's Cafe operates 08+ thriving outlets across 03+ cities in Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh, serving thousands of handcrafted cups daily.

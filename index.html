THE 9TEAS STORY

MORE THAN A CUP OF CHAI.
A Place To Connect.

Founded in 2019 at Bhayander East, Thane by young entrepreneurs Amit Indrabali Yadav & Rupesh Govind Pendhari and guided by mentor C.A. Prashant Tripathi (Founder of Elite Education), 9Tea’s Cafe was born from a singular passion: reviving the pure nostalgia of Indian tea culture in a vibrant, youth-centric cafe environment.

What started as a cherished local haven quickly grew into Mira-Bhayander's most loved tea destination. With thousands of cups hand-brewed fresh daily across 08+ outlets in 03+ cities, we blend age-old kadak kulhad chai traditions with contemporary cafe delights — from stone-baked pizzas to sizzling pastas and coolers.
